Analysis of social media addiction

Analysis of how age, gender, education, and region influence social media use and its academic impacts.

Applied knowledge: Excel (Pivot tables, XLOOKUP, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIFS, data visualization)

Focus areas: Cleaning and structuring survey data, regional grouping of 111 countries, platform categorization, and visualization of patterns.

Date: maj 2025

Analyzed responses from 705 students. Results suggest teenagers and young adults spend ~5 hours/day on social media, reducing sleep and academic performance. Younger groups were more vulnerable, while gender and relationship status showed little influence. Instagram was the most popular platform overall. Dataset limitations (imbalanced regional and gender representation) reduce generalizability, but the project highlights Excel skills and critical interpretation of survey data.
